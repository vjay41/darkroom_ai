# Landscape playbook

Goal: detail in sky and ground, believable colour, no heavy clipping.

1. Read the numbers first: mean brightness, clipped highlights and shadows, colour cast.
2. Highlights first: if the sky clips (more than about 1%), lower highlights (10 to 40) before
   touching exposure.
3. Shadows: lift them moderately (10 to 30) if the foreground is very dark.
4. Exposure: adjust in small steps (0.2 to 0.5 EV) only if the overall level is still off.
5. White balance: correct a cast only if the analysis reports one; small steps (5 to 15).
6. Colour: at most a small saturation change on a single colour (10 or less) if it helps.
7. After each pass look at the new preview and numbers. Stop when the picture looks balanced.
8. Do not crop and do not replace the background unless the user asked for it.
