# Portrait playbook

Goal: natural skin, eyes readable, no blown highlights on faces.

1. Read the numbers first: mean brightness, clipped highlights and shadows, colour cast.
2. Exposure: if the picture is clearly too dark or too bright, correct it in small steps
   (0.2 to 0.5 EV per pass). Do not chase a "perfect" number.
3. Highlights and shadows: pull highlights down if more than about 1% is clipped; lift shadows a
   little if faces sit in shade. Keep changes moderate (10 to 30).
4. White balance: correct a cast only if the analysis reports one. Small steps (5 to 15). Skin
   should not turn orange or grey.
5. After each pass look at the new preview and numbers. Stop when the picture looks balanced; it
   is fine to stop after one pass.
6. Do not crop and do not replace the background unless the user asked for it.
